The **Shantou AI – AI Agent for Job Application** project created a Chrome extension designed to streamline and personalize the job application process. The extension scrapes job postings and resumes to auto-fill applications with an **85% field-match accuracy**, significantly reducing manual effort for users.  

An integrated AI agent generates tailored responses to application questions by aligning resume content with job descriptions, improving personalization and relevance. In addition, a user dashboard was built to support **resume variant customization and submission history tracking**, providing a seamless end-to-end solution for job seekers.
