I am a co-founder of 1Shan AI, where we are developing a General-Purpose Aesthetic Foundation Model that evaluates and enhances visual aesthetics using AI. Our model provides absolute aesthetic scoring for single images and relative ranking for multiple images, unlocking new capabilities in AI-driven image enhancement and personalized content retrieval.  

🔹 Enhancement: Our model improves the quality of text-to-image generation through reinforcement learning fine-tuning—optimizing outputs just like GPT-o1 or DeepSeek R1 but for aesthetics.  

🔹 Personalized Retrieval: Enables efficient discovery of aesthetically appealing images, clothing, and other visually-driven products based on aesthetic scores.  

🚀 Current Progress:  

* Developed a large-scale aesthetics dataset for model training.  
* Built a working prototype for scoring, ranking, and retrieval.  
* Exploring applications in fashion, design, and AI-generated media.  
* Our vision is to define aesthetic reasoning in AI, ensuring that generated visuals meet the highest standards of beauty and personalization.
